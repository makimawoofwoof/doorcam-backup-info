Doorcam Backup

Doorcam Backup is a personal application for backing up its owner's private camera recordings and application data to the owner's Google Drive.

Google Drive access is used to upload backups, find and retrieve saved files, and manage backup retention. Access is authorized by the owner through Google.

This information website contains no camera feed, recordings, sign-in form, or access to the private application. The application is for personal use and is not offered as a public service.
